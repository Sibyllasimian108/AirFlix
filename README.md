## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Video Won't Play?
- Try a different source – click the "Sources" button under the player.
- Check your internet connection.
- Restart the app.

### App Looks Blurry?
- Go to Settings (gear icon).
- Increase the video quality setting.
- Make sure your screen resolution is set correctly in Windows.

### Subtitles Missing?
- Click the "CC" icon in the player.
- Select your preferred subtitle language.
- If no subtitles appear, try a different video source.

### Downloads Stuck?
- Pause and resume the download.
- Check available storage space on your drive.
- Try downloading one item at a time.
